# Warranty Policy

Northgate Water Systems · Effective 1 January 2026

---

## 1. Coverage

| Equipment | Parts | Labour |
|---|---|---|
| NG-4200 and NG-4800 softeners — control valve | 5 years | 1 year |
| NG-4200 and NG-4800 softeners — resin tank | 10 years | 1 year |
| NG-RN radon aeration — tank and blower | 5 years | 1 year |
| NG-RN radon aeration — repressurisation pump | 3 years | 1 year |
| Carbon filtration — media tank | 7 years | 1 year |
| Carbon filtration — media | Not covered — consumable | — |
| Ultraviolet — chamber | 3 years | 1 year |
| Ultraviolet — lamp and sleeve | 1 year | — |
| Sediment cartridges, salt, resin cleaner | Not covered — consumable | — |

Cover runs from the installation date recorded on the job, not the purchase
date. Where the two differ by more than 90 days, the purchase date applies.

---

## 2. Conditions

Warranty cover requires **all** of the following:

- Installation by Northgate or by an authorised dealer.
- Annual service performed and recorded. A missed year does not void cover, but
  a claim arising from a condition that annual service would have caught is
  declined.
- Feed water within the published operating limits for the equipment.
- Genuine replacement parts and media.

---

## 3. What Is Not Covered

- Damage from freezing. This is the most frequent decline, and it is not
  arguable — an unheated space is the property owner's responsibility.
- Iron or manganese fouling on a system specified without iron pre-treatment
  where the water test showed iron present. The specification error is a
  commercial matter, handled outside warranty.
- Salt bridging, and any consequence of it.
- Damage from a pressure surge where no pressure-reducing valve is fitted and
  the static pressure exceeds 80 psi.
- Any equipment relocated by a third party.
- Cosmetic marks that do not affect function.
- **Water temperature change following a radon aeration installation.** This is
  inherent to the process and is documented in the NG-RN manual. It is not a
  fault and is not a warranty matter.

---

## 4. Making a Claim

The office opens the claim; technicians do not file directly. Required:

1. The serial number and the installation date.
2. The most recent service record.
3. A current water test where the claim concerns fouling or capacity.
4. Photographs of the failed component in place, before removal.

Approved claims are usually resolved within five working days. Where a part is
on back-order, a loan unit is offered on installations less than two years old.

---

## 5. Goodwill

Outside warranty, the branch manager may authorise parts at cost plus labour at
half rate, once per customer. This is a judgement, not an entitlement, and it is
not to be offered to a customer by a technician on site — refer it to the
office.
