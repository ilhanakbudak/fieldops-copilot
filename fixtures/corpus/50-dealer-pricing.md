## 2. Margin Floor

Gross margin below **38 percent** requires branch manager approval before the
estimate is issued. Below 30 percent it is declined regardless of volume — at
that level a single warranty callback erases the job's contribution entirely.

Bundle discounting: a second unit on the same visit may be discounted up to 8
percent, because the labour is largely shared. A third unit does not earn a
further discount; the labour saving is already spent.

## 5. Supplier Terms

Primary supplier is Halstead Fluid Supply: net 30, 2 percent for payment within
10 days, free freight above $2,500. Secondary is Coventry Water Components,
used when Halstead lead times exceed two weeks — roughly 6 percent more
expensive and worth it against a delayed installation.
