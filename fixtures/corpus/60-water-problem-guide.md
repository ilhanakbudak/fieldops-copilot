# Water Problem to Treatment Guide

Northgate Water Systems · For diagnosis from a customer description

---

## How to Use This

A customer describes a symptom, not a water chemistry problem. This maps the
former to the latter. **Nothing here substitutes for a water test** — it tells
you what to test for and what to expect.

---

## Scale on Fixtures, Poor Lather, Spotted Glassware

**Hardness.** Calcium and magnesium above roughly 7 grains per gallon.

Treatment: ion-exchange softener, sized on hardness times daily consumption.
NG-4200 up to 25 gpg for a typical four-person household; NG-4800 above that or
for larger households.

Test for iron before specifying. Iron above 1.0 ppm fouls softener resin, and a
softener installed on iron-bearing water without pre-treatment will fail early
and outside warranty.

---

## Orange or Brown Staining

**Ferric iron** — already oxidised, visible as particles.

Treatment: air-injection iron filter, NG-IR. A sediment filter alone will clog
within weeks.

---

## Clear Water That Stains After Standing

**Ferrous iron** — dissolved, oxidises on contact with air.

Treatment: air-injection oxidising filter. A softener will remove modest amounts
as a secondary effect but is not the right primary treatment above 0.3 ppm.

---

## Black Specks, Grey Staining

**Manganese.** Frequently accompanies iron and is treated the same way, but
oxidises more slowly — size the contact tank generously.

---

## Rotten Egg Smell

**Hydrogen sulphide.** Establish first whether it is on the hot side only: if
so, the source is the water heater's sacrificial anode, and replacing the anode
with an aluminium-zinc type solves it without any treatment equipment.

On both hot and cold: air-injection oxidising filter, or continuous chlorination
with carbon on higher concentrations.

---

## Chlorine Taste or Smell

**Municipal disinfection residual.**

Treatment: carbon filtration, NG-CF. Also protects downstream softener resin,
which chlorine degrades.

---

## Bacteriological Failure

**Coliform present.**

Treatment: shock chlorination of the well first, then re-test. If it fails
again, ultraviolet disinfection sized to peak flow, with sediment pre-filtration
— UV cannot disinfect water it cannot see through.

---

## Radon in Water

**Waterborne radon above 4,000 pCi/L**, or above the state action level where
one applies.

Treatment: NG-RN spray aeration. Granular activated carbon is an alternative at
low concentrations but accumulates radioactivity in the media and creates a
disposal problem; we do not specify it.

Set the expectation at the estimate stage that cold water will run warmer after
installation.

---

## Blue-Green Staining

**Low pH**, dissolving copper from the plumbing.

Treatment: acid-neutralising filter with calcite. Raises hardness as a side
effect, so it usually pairs with a softener.

---

## Cloudy Water That Clears From the Bottom

Air entrainment, not a contaminant. Check the well pump for a suction-side leak.
No treatment equipment is required and none should be sold.
